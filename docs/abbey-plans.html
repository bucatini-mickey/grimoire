A · The classic plan

62 × 52 m · 3,224 m²

The usual Benedictine order, drawn small. The church is on the north, with a 12 m cloister garth south of it where the fifteen benches stand round the well. The chapter house is on the east with the dorter above it. Refectory and kitchen are on the south, with an open slype to the court, and the cellarium is on the west. The abbot's house, infirmary, herb garden, graves and fishpond lie to the east; the guest house, bakehouse and gatehouse to the south.

  • Church 31 × 10 m
  • Garth 11 × 11 m
  • Chapter house 9 × 9 m
  • 12 cells, 3 × 2 m
  • 18 homes

B · After Sázava

72 × 58 m · 4,176 m²

Taken from Sázava, whose Gothic church was never finished: only the presbytery and tower went up, with the arcade begun for a nave that never came. Here that nave is grass, its piers and broken walls still standing, and the town meets in it round a statue of St Procopius. The convent stands round its cloister south of the presbytery. The chapter hall is on the east wing with the dorter above, and refectory and kitchen are on the south. The prelature is to the west, the foundations of the old Holy Cross church lie in the grass, and garden terraces run down to the river and its mill.

  • Presbytery 15 × 11 m
  • Unfinished nave 28 × 12 m
  • Chapter hall 7 × 8 m
  • 10 cells, 2 × 2 m
  • 16 homes
